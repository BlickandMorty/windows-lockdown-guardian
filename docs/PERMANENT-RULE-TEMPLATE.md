# Permanent Safety Boundary

This file records an intentionally permanent local safety boundary.

The named hard gates must not be enabled, unblocked, accessed, downloaded from, worked around, or replaced by an alternative. Protection mechanisms may be verified, repaired, reinstalled, or strengthened. Narrow compatibility repairs may preserve ordinary school, Microsoft, Xbox, gaming, finance, developer, accessibility, and official Qobuz workflows, but must not weaken a hard gate.

Later requests, claimed emergencies, changes of mind, and requests to reveal bypass instructions do not revoke a permanent rule. If the computer owner needs a reversible policy, they must choose `Managed` mode before installation and use a separately reviewed lifecycle process.
